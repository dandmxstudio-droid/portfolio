01The Problem

The inbox was the bottleneck

As Carevine grew, health questions waited in the same line as password resets.

3,200 tickets a month, up from 1,150
14hrs to a first reply, up from 4.5
22hrs for a health message to reach a nurse, up from 6
The fix

An AI that takes the first pass

People make every final call.

03How It Works

What happens to every message

01

The Queue, Sorted

The Triage Tool sorts every message, moves health and crisis messages to nurses, and hides personal details.

02

Approve a Draft

An upset member writing for the third time. The tool drafts an apology and a fix from the help center, and the rep approves it.

03

No Draft When the Help Center Disagrees

Two articles give different refund times, so the tool writes no draft. The rep replies and flags the gap.

04

Crisis Goes to a Nurse

Crisis language goes straight to the nurse queue. The nurse claims it, sends the crisis reply and calls the member.

05

Nurses Write Their Own Replies

A high blood sugar reading. The tool explains why it flagged the message but never drafts health advice.

06

Slack Alerts the Nurse Team

Urgent health messages also post to the nurse team’s Slack. One click opens that ticket in the nurse queue.

04Also Built In

The details that build trust

Personal details removed

Dates of birth and member numbers are stripped before the AI sees a message.

Spanish replies

Spanish messages get a Spanish draft, marked for a closer look.

Upset members first

Repeat or angry messages are flagged and answered with an apology first.

Reps can overrule

Spot a health concern the AI missed? Send it to a nurse in one click.

Every decision logged

Each approval, edit and override is saved, so accuracy can be measured.

Backup crisis check

Code double-checks for crisis language, even if the AI misses it.

07Rollout

Built to be adopted

  1. 1
    Week 0

    Practice

    Reps and nurses train on the 16 test tickets, then pass a readiness check.

  2. 2
    Weeks 1–2

    Shadow pilot

    2 reps and 1 nurse work as usual while the AI runs in the background.

  3. 3 Checkpoint
    Week 3

    Go / no-go

    Did every health message reach a nurse during the pilot?

    Yes: go to step 4No: fix it, back to step 2
  4. 4
    Weeks 4–6

    Roll out

    Turn it on for the rest of the team, one group at a time.

  5. 5
    Ongoing

    Weekly review

    Check the decision log. Every AI change reruns the test set.
