Patient Message Triage

A working AI tool that sorts a virtual-care support inbox, gets health messages to nurses in seconds, and drafts replies reps approve. Built end to end and passed all 16 test tickets, backed by 76 automated tests.

02 / 03 Banking · AI Agent

AI Fraud Investigator

An AI agent built on Jev that investigates bank fraud alerts, closes the clear false alarms and sends the rest to an analyst with a recommendation. Tested on 50 held-out alerts: 45 right, no fraud closed alone, at a third of the cost of a single AI model.

Training Content at Scale

An AI-automated system that scripts and produces a complete microlearning course in about 2 hours, with a person approving every script and video. 13 courses at 50–70% lower cost, piloted with 6 clinics.

What I Build

AI Agents

Internal AI Tools

Tools that sort, route and draft routine work for support and ops teams to review.

Workflow Automations

AI Feature Prototypes

Discovery & Strategy

Research that finds where AI earns its place, then scopes and ranks what to build first.

AI UX Design

How AI asks, shows how sure it is, recovers from errors and hands off to a person.

Evals & Testing

Test sets that show where AI is right, where it fails and what each decision costs.

AI Enablement
