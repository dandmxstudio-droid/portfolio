01Problem

AI was coming into clinics.

No one was training care teams to use it safely.

What I saw

At a Fortune 500 company, I did field research in clinics across the US and saw care teams stretched between technology, workload and regulations like HIPAA.

The bet

Small clinics would need practical AI safety training.

To test it, I needed real courses at scale, without a production budget.

Traditional: Chapman Alliance benchmark, 49–79 hours per finished hour of basic e-learning, at $33.31/hour (BLS 2025 median, training and development specialists). This system includes a full year of tools on annual plans.

The result

50–70% lower cost, and a library that keeps up when the rules change.

Rules keep moving

California AB 3030 already requires disclaimers on AI-generated patient messages, and a major HIPAA Security Rule update is on the way.
