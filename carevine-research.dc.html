04Design rules

From findings to design rules

Six rules for the Triage Tool, each traced to a finding.

  1. 01Health questions go to a person, fast. AI routes them and never answers them.People reserve medication and health questions for humans; health messages waited 22 hours.
  2. 02A person checks before anything reaches a member.89% say it’s important for a human to check AI health answers.
  3. 03Show where every answer came from.69% worry about inaccurate AI answers.
  4. 04Say ‘I don’t know’ instead of guessing.14 of 100 tickets hit help center gaps.
  5. 05Send AI only what it needs.74% worry about health data privacy.
  6. 06Treat crisis language as an emergency.Crisis moments show up in ordinary app reviews and messages.
